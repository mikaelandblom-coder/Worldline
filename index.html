A game on a 1+1D spacetime diagram · c = 1

Worldline

You are an unstable particle with a short life. Reach the exit before your own clock runs out. Moving fast makes that clock tick slower, but you can never cross the light cone.

The rules

v = tanh φ
Thrust changes your rapidity φ, so speed never reaches 1.
dτ = dt / cosh φ
Your clock runs slow when you move fast.
D = e±φ
Light you run into is blueshifted and worth more fuel.
